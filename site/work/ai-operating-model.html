Axolt · How we use AI

A two-person company with AI as the team

Axolt is a small supplement brand. Two founders run it, and AI does most of the research, writing, analysis and design. The founders decide what happens and approve anything that goes out. Everything the AI learns is kept in one shared knowledge base, so it gets better every week.

17AI agents, each with its own job
30+business tools the AI can work in
43repeatable tasks the AI runs on request or on a schedule
6weeks to build, starting from nothing

01 · How it works

Four layers: the tools, the brain, the agents and the human

Information flows in from the tools we already use and is stored in one shared brain. A team of AI agents uses that brain to do the work, and a founder approves the result. Every correction is saved, so the system keeps learning.

01

Marketing

Deciding what to say to customers, where to say it, and making sure we're allowed to say it.

The founder's partChooses the message and approves the final copy.

  • Reviewing every ad we've run

    The AI went through all 616 image ads we've run on Facebook and Instagram and worked out which messages and styles actually led to sales.

  • Collecting competitor ads

    It gathers competitors' ads into one library we can search for ideas and turn into templates.

  • Writing landing pages

    It drafts the web pages people see after clicking an ad, including longer article-style pages for people who have never heard of us.

  • Defining who we're for

    It combined customer feedback, support emails and research into our brand positioning and brand guidelines.

  • Briefing content creators

    It writes the briefs we send to creators who make videos for us, and plans ad tests for different customer groups.

  • Checking health claims

    Supplement advertising has strict rules. The AI checks every sentence and marks what we can say, per country and per channel.

02

Creative & design

New images, product photos and web page designs, without a photo shoot for every idea.

The founder's partPicks the best results and says why.

  • Generating ad images

    The AI creates new ad images and photography in our brand style with an AI image generator (Higgsfield), several options per idea.

  • Product mockups

    Realistic photos of new packaging, front, back and held in a hand, before anything is printed.

  • Designing web pages

    It lays out product and landing pages following our brand's design rules in Figma.

  • Learning our taste

    Every image we keep or reject is recorded with the reason, so each new batch is closer to what we want.

03

Ad performance

Knowing which ads make money, using the same numbers every month.

The founder's partDecides the budget and what to test next.

  • runs automatically · monthly

    Monthly performance report

    Sales from the shop, ad results from Facebook and Google, and website traffic go into one spreadsheet, and the AI checks the totals add up.

  • Ad account check-ups

    It works out which ads, audiences and messages bring in customers at a profitable cost.

  • Making sure tracking works

    It checks that every sale is reported correctly back to the ad platforms and to Google Analytics.

  • Learning from past discussions

    It read our team's old chat threads about ads and listed what we tried, what worked and the mistakes we keep repeating.

  • Planning promotions

    It researched how competitors run Black Friday and recommended our offer.

04

Market research

Knowing what competitors are doing and where the next customers are.

The founder's partDecides positioning and price.

  • runs automatically · daily

    Daily competitor watch

    Every morning the AI checks competitors' websites and ads for price changes, new products, new ads and news.

  • Competitor deep dives

    For key rivals it estimates revenue, where their sales come from, which ads work for them and where they are weak.

  • Price comparison

    It collected competitors' prices and subscription deals, compared them with ours and recommended our offer.

  • Finding new markets

    It sized the UK market for women with brain fog during menopause, and ranked ten other groups by how likely they are to pay.

  • runs automatically · weekly

    Weekly AI news

    It filters the week's AI news down to the few updates that matter to us.

05

E-commerce & website

We no longer need a developer. The AI writes the code and changes the online shop itself.

The founder's partApproves every change before it goes live.

  • Updating the shop

    The AI changes products, prices, collections and discount codes in Shopify directly.

  • Building new pages

    It designs and codes new landing pages and product page sections, ready to publish.

  • Writing code

    Website features, the subscription price display and small scripts are written and tested by the AI.

  • Running A/B tests

    It builds two versions of a page, splits visitors between them and reports which one sells more.

  • Fixing problems fast

    When a test finds an error, like a wrong price on the homepage, the AI writes the fix the same day.

  • Shop automations

    It sets up the rules that run the shop in the background, like tagging orders and sending alerts.

06

Data & analysis

An analyst on call. Any question about the business, answered from the real numbers.

The founder's partAsks the question and acts on the answer.

  • Answering business questions

    Questions like "how many customers buy a second time?" are answered straight from shop and subscription data.

  • Customer value

    How much a subscriber is worth over time, how long they stay, and how that changes month to month.

  • Live dashboards

    Interactive pages that read current data, such as the partner pipeline and the competitor overview.

  • Product trial results

    Before-and-after survey results from test customers, showing who improved and by how much.

  • Survey analysis

    Hundreds of customer survey answers grouped into clear themes and numbers.

07

Operations

Orders, stock and shipping across four warehouses, watched every day.

The founder's partFixes what's flagged and confirms orders.

  • runs automatically · daily

    Morning order check

    The AI checks the warehouse system and the shop for stuck orders and low stock, and posts a summary in the team chat.

  • Sending free samples

    It creates and tracks orders for partners, creators and people testing the product.

  • Running product trials

    It manages a group of test customers: shipping, surveys on day 12 and day 24, and reminders.

  • Warehouse costs

    It mapped how orders move between our four warehouses and explained the shipping bill line by line.

  • Testing before launch

    It tests new web pages on phone and desktop: prices, links and checkout. One test found 7 serious problems before customers saw them.

08

Customer service & retention

Every customer question answered, every piece of feedback read, and customers kept for longer.

The founder's partChecks the drafts and sends them.

  • runs automatically · daily

    Answering customer questions

    The AI drafts a reply to every support request from our guidelines. A person checks it and sends it.

  • Customer emails

    It drafts email replies in Gmail, inside the same conversation, ready to send.

  • runs automatically · daily

    Morning feedback summary

    It reads new support requests, reviews and survey answers, and highlights what needs attention.

  • Learning from customer calls

    It summarises recorded customer calls and interviews into why people buy, hesitate or cancel.

  • Automated emails

    It rewrote our welcome, after-purchase and win-back email sequences, ready for approval.

  • Learning our tone

    It compares its drafts with what we actually sent, and adjusts how it writes.

09

Partnerships

Finding clinics, nutritionists and retailers to sell through, and following up.

The founder's partSends the messages and takes the meetings.

  • Finding partners

    The AI researched 127 UK longevity clinics and added the best fits to our contact database.

  • runs automatically · daily

    Keeping contacts up to date

    It logs every partner email, updates where each deal stands, and posts today's follow-ups.

  • Taking over a colleague's contacts

    When a team member left, it went through their inbox, drafted 41 follow-up emails and added 20 new contacts.

  • Preparing for events

    Before a conference it lists who to meet and drafts the invitations, so meetings are booked in advance.

10

Finance & pricing

Clear numbers on margins and costs, without rebuilding spreadsheets by hand.

The founder's partMakes the pricing and spending decisions.

  • Pricing calculator

    An interactive model that shows our profit for every price, country, discount and wholesale deal.

  • Pricing a new product

    Profit at every possible shelf price, worked out before launch.

  • Costs, invoices and bank

    It explains costs line by line, creates invoices and reads bank transactions. It cannot move money.

11

Internal updates & knowledge

Nothing said in a meeting, chat or email gets lost.

The founder's partApproves what gets saved.

  • runs automatically · hourly

    Meeting notes into decisions

    Every recorded meeting is reduced to its decisions and to-dos, and saved after approval.

  • Old AI chats into knowledge

    Months of earlier conversations with AI were turned into 45 organised documents in the brain.

  • runs automatically · daily

    Team updates in Slack

    Order checks, partner follow-ups and the AI news digest are posted to the team chat.

  • One task list

    Every request to the AI is tracked with who owns it, its status and the next step.

04 · Our rules

The AI prepares. People decide.

Drafts only

The AI never sends an email, publishes a page or spends money on its own.

Real numbers

Figures come from our systems and the brain, never from the AI's memory.

Claims checked

Every sentence a customer will read is checked against advertising rules.

Nothing private stored

No passwords or customer personal details are kept in the brain.
