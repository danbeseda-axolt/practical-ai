The problem: silos

Work was duplicated or never shared. When it was shared, it went round Slack as Excel, PowerPoint and Markdown files, and nobody knew which version was current. Meetings ran in different software, so what was decided in them was mostly lost.

What I built

One repository holding everything the company knows, open to everyone who needs it.

Meetings & chats

Call transcripts, Slack threads and the decisions made in them.

What we've built

Brand manuals, playbooks, product docs and SOPs.

Research

New findings on the market, competitors and our category.

Customers

Feedback from tickets, reviews, surveys and conversations.

What changed

  • One source of truth. No more chasing the latest deck or file.
  • Nothing gets lost. Every meeting, research run and customer conversation adds to it.
  • People and agents share it. An agent answering a customer uses the same playbook a person would.
  • Same tools for everyone. Skills, agents, prompts and commands live there too, so a new hire has what the most experienced person has.
