## Context

What was happening that made this a question. Enough that someone reading it in
a year, who was not there, understands the pressure. Name the specific thing
that was breaking, not the general area.

## Options considered

1. **Do nothing.** Always list this one. It is often the right answer and it
   sets the bar the others have to clear.
2. **[Option].**
3. **[Option].** *(chosen)*

## Decision

What was decided, stated so a reader can act on it without interpretation.

## Why

The reasoning, in the order that actually persuaded you. Not a balanced essay.

### What we traded away

What this costs, named honestly. A decision with no downside was not a decision.

### The risk that matters

The one that would hurt, and what stops it.

## Revisit when

Concrete triggers, not "in six months". A number crossing a threshold, a tool
changing, a person leaving, an assumption turning out to be wrong.
